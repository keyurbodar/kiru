## Goal
Every order needs the user's explicit yes before money moves. This PR puts the `approvalUrl` from the `send_calls` flow into a live price/approval card in the Photon chat thread, so approve-or-decline is one tap.

## Scope
- Touched: `eve/agent/channels/photon.ts` (approval card send + reply handling)
- Touched: `eve/agent/tools/executeBuy.ts` (expose `approvalUrl` to the channel layer)

## Steps
1. Build the approval card payload in `photon.ts`: token, side, USDC amount, venue quote summary, `approvalUrl` link (4 min).
2. Send the card through the Photon channel into the same iMessage thread right after `quoteBuy` resolves (3 min).
3. Handle the user's approve reply by continuing into `executeBuy`; handle decline by cancelling the HOT pending quote (3 min).
4. Verify by texting "buy $50 of NVDAc" in the Photon dev thread, tapping the approval link, and confirming approve continues and decline cancels (4 min).

## Acceptance
- Approval card with working `approvalUrl` link appears in chat after every quote.
- Approve continues to `executeBuy`; decline cancels the HOT pending quote.
- Nothing executes without the user's tap.
