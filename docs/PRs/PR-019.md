## Goal
A weekly "here's your money" text keeps users engaged without opening an app. This PR adds the Eve scheduled recap job that summarizes positions, basis, and recent fills from WARM entities and the COLD fills journal into one Photon message.

## Scope
- Created: `eve/agent/schedules/recap.ts` (weekly schedule, summary compose)
- Touched: `eve/agent/channels/photon.ts` (recap delivery send)

## Steps
1. Read WARM position/basis entities via `recall` and recent fills from the COLD journal via `search` (4 min).
2. Compose the recap text: holdings with basis, week's fills, total USDC on Base 8453 (4 min).
3. Schedule weekly delivery through `photon.ts`, skipping quietly when the user holds nothing and filled nothing (3 min).
4. Verify by seeding mock entities and fills, triggering the schedule manually, and confirming the recap text matches stored state (3 min).

## Acceptance
- Weekly recap delivers via Photon with holdings, basis, and week's fills.
- Numbers match WARM entities and COLD journal reads.
- Empty portfolios/fill-less weeks send nothing.
